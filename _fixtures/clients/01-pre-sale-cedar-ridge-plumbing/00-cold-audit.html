Conversion audit

Where Cedar Ridge Plumbing is quietly losing clients

The site is clear about what you do and where you do it — but here is where enquiries are going missing before they ever reach you.

Before you read any of this

Everything in this document was read from the outside — your public pages, on a scan, without anyone speaking to you. That is the smaller half of the problem.

The bigger half sits inside how enquiries get handled once they arrive: how fast someone gets back, who chases the ones that go quiet, what happens to the people who never book. A scan cannot see any of that, and this document is not going to pretend it can. Those are the questions for the call.

Your single biggest leak

You already pay to get these leads, through referrals and the time you put into being found. Every one that hits this gap is money you have already spent to earn somebody who now slips away before they reach you.

Just one of 4 leaks we found below — and the most expensive of them.

What a scan can see from out here
01

There is no way to book you without a phone call

Critical

Measured on your public pages, 7/28/2026

Neither the site nor your Google profile carries a booking link, so every appointment has to go through a conversation while somebody in the office is free to have one.

What it's costing youA homeowner who decides at 10pm has to remember to ring in the morning, and remembering is where a lot of that intent quietly dies.
02

Every enquiry arrives looking exactly the same

Critical

Measured on your public pages, 7/28/2026

The contact form collects a name, an email, a phone number and a message box. Nothing on it says what the job is, where it is, or how urgent it has become.

What it's costing youA full re-pipe gets read in the same order as a dripping tap, so the biggest job of the week routinely waits the longest.
03

A call that rings out has nothing behind it

Critical

Industry pattern — not measured for you

The published number is a single line with no text-back path anywhere on the site, so what happens to a call nobody picks up is not something a scan can see from out here.

What it's costing youWhere that pattern holds, a caller who cannot get through typically dials the next company within minutes, and nothing brings them back.
04

Both buttons on the page need the office to be open

Costly

Measured on your public pages, 7/28/2026

The two calls to action above the fold are a request-a-quote link to the contact form and a tap-to-call link to the main line, and neither produces a result outside office hours.

What it's costing youA visitor who arrives with a problem in front of them at 9pm has nothing on the page that completes.
