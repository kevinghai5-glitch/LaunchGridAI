What We Install

13 of 14 automations are built into your account and handed over working. Each one runs on its own — none of them depends on somebody remembering.

Instant Lead Response

Every new enquiry gets an answer inside about a minute, day or night, without anyone having to notice it came in. The reply comes from your business number and your email address, and it carries a link to book.

What you will see. A text and a matching email within about a minute: "Thanks for getting in touch with [Business] — we have your details and someone is on it. Want to lock in a time now? [booking link]". The enquiry appears in your pipeline at New Lead with the time it arrived.

Missed Call Text-Back

When a call rings out or drops into voicemail, the caller gets a text straight back instead of silence. Most people answer a text, so the conversation carries on from there rather than moving to the next business on their list.

What you will see. The caller gets: "Sorry we missed you — this is [Business]. Tell us what you need and we will sort it, or grab a time here: [booking link]". You get a note of who called with their number, and they land in your pipeline at New Lead.

After-Hours Auto-Reply

Calls and messages that arrive when you are closed get an answer instead of a closed door. The reply says when you open, takes their details, and offers them a time on the calendar while they are still thinking about it.

What you will see. "Thanks for reaching [Business] — we are closed right now and open again at [time]. Leave us the details and we will call first thing, or book yourself in here: [booking link]". Anything that reads as urgent is flagged to you rather than held until morning.

Booking Confirmation + Reminders

Every booking is confirmed the moment it is made, then reminded before it happens. The customer knows where to be and when, and you stop losing slots to people who simply forgot.

What you will see. A confirmation text and email with the date, time and address plus a calendar invite, then a reminder a day out and another two hours out. Each one carries a link to move the appointment, so a clash becomes a reschedule instead of an empty slot.

Appointment Cancelled — Stop Reminders

The moment an appointment is cancelled or moved, the reminders for the old slot stop. Nobody gets a cheerful reminder for a visit they already cancelled.

What you will see. Silence about the old slot — that is the whole job. What they do get is one short note confirming the cancellation with a link to pick a new time, and if they rebook, the reminders start again against the new appointment.

No-Show Recovery

When somebody does not turn up, they hear from you within the hour with an easy way to rebook, rather than the slot quietly becoming a write-off.

What you will see. "We had you down for [time] today and missed you — no problem at all. Pick a new time here: [booking link]". One more nudge follows the next day if nothing comes back, and then they move into the follow-up sequence rather than being dropped.

Review Request

Every finished job asks the customer for a Google review at the moment they are happiest with you, without anyone having to remember to do it.

What you will see. "Thanks for having us out, [name]. If we did right by you, a quick review helps us more than you would think: [review link]". One polite reminder follows if nothing happens, and then it stops asking.

Lead Nurture — No Booking

A lead who never booked keeps hearing from you for 60 days — a handful of texts and emails spread out, not a barrage. If they book or reply it stops on its own; if the 60 days run out, the deal moves to Lost so your pipeline shows what is real.

What you will see. A short run of texts and emails over 60 days — a nudge, a reminder of what you do, a straight "do you still want that quote?" — each with a booking link, each stopping the moment they reply or book.

Owner Hot-Lead Notification

The enquiries worth dropping everything for reach you the second they land, separately from everything else, so a big job never sits in the queue behind ten small ones.

What you will see. A text and a phone notification: who it is, what they asked for, why it scored hot, and their number ready to tap and call. The lead sits at the top of your pipeline until somebody touches it.

Social DM Capture

Messages sent to your Facebook page or Instagram account land in the same place as your calls and forms, get the same quick first reply, and become contacts you can follow up like any other enquiry.

What you will see. The sender gets a reply in the app within a minute with a link to book, and you see their message alongside your texts and emails instead of in a separate phone app nobody opens between jobs.

Text-to-Pay

When a customer says yes, they get a link by text and pay while they still feel like saying yes. Paying holds the slot, and the receipt and the booking confirmation go out on their own.

What you will see. A text with the amount and a link — card details on their phone, done in under a minute, receipt straight after. The deal moves to Won in your pipeline once the payment clears.

Database Reactivation

Your past customers and old quotes hear from you again — a short, polite campaign that brings some of them back for work they were already going to need.

What you will see. "Hi [name], it is [Business]. It has been a while since we [job] for you. If you are about due, here is a time: [booking link]", with a one-tap way to hear nothing further. Anyone who replies lands in your pipeline as a new lead.

Review Response

Every new Google review gets a reply, quickly and in your voice, whether it is five stars or one. Reviews sitting there unanswered read as inattentive to the next person deciding whether to call you.

What you will see. A reply under each new review, usually the same day — warm and specific on a good one, calm and straight on a complaint — posted in your business name, with anything awkward run past you first.

What Is Not Installed, and Why

Pending. This one needs a small snippet added to your website. Whether that is possible depends on how your site is built, so we confirm it during the build rather than promising it now.
