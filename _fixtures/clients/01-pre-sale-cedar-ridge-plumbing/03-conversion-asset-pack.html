FAQ

How quickly can somebody get here?
Urgent no hot water calls are usually same day. Everything else is normally within the same week, and you will be given a real window rather than a vague morning or afternoon.

What does it cost to have somebody look at it?
There is a callout fee for the visit, quoted to you before it is booked, and it comes off the bill if you go ahead with the work. Nothing starts without a written price you have agreed to.

What happens if I message you in the evening?
You get an acknowledgement within a minute confirming we have it, and a real reply first thing. If it is an emergency, the message routes to the on-call plumber rather than waiting for the office to open.

Which areas do you cover?
Nanaimo and the mid-Island. If you are outside that we will say so straight away rather than leaving you waiting on a callback.

Should I repair the hot water tank or replace it?
That depends on age, what has failed and what the repair costs against a replacement. The plumber will give you both numbers and tell you honestly which one they would choose.

The page does the honest things well: the headline says what you do and where, and the phone number is a real tap-to-call link in the HTML rather than typed-out digits.
