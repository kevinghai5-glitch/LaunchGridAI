01
They reach out

Somebody gets in touch — a call, a form, a chat, a message on Facebook or Instagram — and hears back within about a minute, whatever time of day it is.

Instant Lead Response
TextEmail
Fires when A form is submitted — on your website, on your booking page, or anywhere else the form is placed.

Every new enquiry gets an answer inside about a minute, day or night, without anyone having to notice it came in. The reply comes from your business number and your email address, and it carries a link to book.

What you will see. A text and a matching email within about a minute: "Thanks for getting in touch with Harbourline Electric — we have your details and someone is on it. Want to lock in a time now? {{custom_values.booking_link}}". The enquiry appears in your pipeline at New Lead with the time it arrived.

Missed Call Text-Back
Text
Fires when A call to your business number rings out unanswered, or lands in voicemail.

When a call rings out or drops into voicemail, the caller gets a text straight back instead of silence. Most people answer a text, so the conversation carries on from there rather than moving to the next business on their list.

What you will see. The caller gets: "Sorry we missed you — this is Harbourline Electric. Tell us what you need and we will sort it, or grab a time here: {{custom_values.booking_link}}". You get a note of who called with their number, and they land in your pipeline at New Lead.

After-Hours Auto-Reply
TextEmail
Fires when A call or a message arrives outside your opening hours — an evening, a weekend, a holiday.

Calls and messages that arrive when you are closed get an answer instead of a closed door. The reply says when you open, takes their details, and offers them a time on the calendar while they are still thinking about it.

What you will see. "Thanks for reaching Harbourline Electric — we are closed right now and open again at {{custom_values.opening_time}}. Leave us the details and we will call first thing, or book yourself in here: {{custom_values.booking_link}}". Anything that reads as urgent is flagged to you rather than held until morning.

Webchat Capture
Fires when A visitor opens the chat box on your website and leaves a name and a number.

A visitor with a quick question can start a conversation from your website instead of leaving. The chat turns into a text thread on your business number, so it carries on after they close the tab.

Confirmed during the build. This one needs a small snippet added to your website. Whether that is possible depends on how your site is built, so we confirm it during the build rather than promising it now.

Social DM Capture
DM
Fires when Somebody sends a message to your Facebook page or your Instagram account.

Messages sent to your Facebook page or Instagram account land in the same place as your calls and forms, get the same quick first reply, and become contacts you can follow up like any other enquiry.

Included by decision. This one is a choice rather than a default — it comes out when this is true: No social accounts at all. That is not you, so it is in.

What you will see. The sender gets a reply in the app within a minute with a link to book, and you see their message alongside your texts and emails instead of in a separate phone app nobody opens between jobs.

02
We qualify

You find out what they want and how urgent it is, and the ones worth dropping everything for reach you separately from the rest.

Owner Hot-Lead Notification
Owner
Fires when A new enquiry scores as high intent — the job type, the urgency or the size crosses the line you set with us.

The enquiries worth dropping everything for reach you the second they land, separately from everything else, so the big ones never sit in the queue behind ten small ones.

What you will see. A text and a phone notification: who it is, what they asked for, why it scored hot, and their number ready to tap and call. The lead sits at the top of your pipeline until somebody touches it.

03
They book

A time goes on the calendar, and everything that keeps it there happens without anyone having to chase it.

Booking Confirmation + Reminders
EmailText
Fires when An appointment is booked — by the customer online, or by you or your team.

Every booking is confirmed the moment it is made, then reminded before it happens. The customer knows where to be and when, and you stop losing slots to people who simply forgot.

What you will see. A confirmation text and email with the date, time and address plus a calendar invite, then a reminder a day out and another two hours out. Each one carries a link to move the appointment, so a clash becomes a reschedule instead of an empty slot.

Appointment Cancelled — Stop Reminders
Text
Fires when An appointment is cancelled or rescheduled, by the customer or by you.

The moment an appointment is cancelled or moved, the reminders for the old slot stop. Nobody gets a cheerful reminder for a visit they already cancelled.

What you will see. Silence about the old slot — that is the whole point. What they do get is one short note confirming the cancellation with a link to pick a new time, and if they rebook, the reminders start again against the new appointment.

Text-to-Pay
Text
Fires when A lead is marked as agreed, or a booking needs a deposit before the slot is held.

When a customer says yes, they get a link by text and pay while they still feel like saying yes. Paying holds the slot, and the receipt and the booking confirmation go out on their own.

Included by decision. This one is a choice rather than a default — it comes out when this is true: Paid at time of service, no deposits. That is not you, so it is in.

What you will see. A text with the amount and a link — card details on their phone, done in under a minute, receipt straight after. The deal moves to Won in your pipeline once the payment clears.

04
They show, or don't

They turn up and get asked for a review, or they do not and get an easy way back onto the calendar.

No-Show Recovery
EmailText
Fires when A booked appointment is marked as a no-show.

When somebody does not turn up, they hear from you within the hour with an easy way to rebook, rather than the slot quietly becoming a write-off.

What you will see. "We had you down for {{appointment.start_time}} today and missed you — no problem at all. Pick a new time here: {{custom_values.booking_link}}". One more nudge follows the next day if nothing comes back, and then they move into the follow-up sequence rather than being dropped.

Review Request
Text
Fires when A job is marked complete — the deal reaches Won in your pipeline.

Every customer is asked for a Google review at the moment they are happiest with you, without anyone having to remember to do it.

What you will see. "Thanks for having us out, {{contact.first_name}}. If we did right by you, a quick review helps us more than you would think: {{custom_values.review_link}}". One polite reminder follows if nothing happens, and then it stops asking.

05
They go quiet

Nothing is dropped for going cold — the lead who never booked, the customer you have not seen in a while, and the review nobody replied to.

Lead Nurture — No Booking
TextEmail
Fires when A new lead passes a set number of days without booking anything.

A lead who never booked keeps hearing from you for 60 days — a handful of texts and emails spread out, not a barrage. If they book or reply it stops on its own; if the 60 days run out, the deal moves to Lost so your pipeline shows what is real.

What you will see. A short run of texts and emails over 60 days — a nudge, a reminder of what you do, a straight "are you still thinking about it?" — each with a booking link, each stopping the moment they reply or book.

Database Reactivation
EmailText
Fires when You hand the list over once and we send it in batches, then it runs again on whatever rhythm you agree — usually seasonal.

Your past customers, and anyone who asked and never booked, hear from you again — a short, polite campaign that brings some of them back for work they were already going to need.

Included by decision. This one is a choice rather than a default — it comes out when this is true: Never or rarely contact past customers. That is not you, so it is in.

What you will see. "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, it is Harbourline Electric. It has been a while since we did your last job. If you are about due, here is a time: {{custom_values.booking_link}}", with a one-tap way to hear nothing further. Anyone who replies lands in your pipeline as a new lead.

Review Response
Review reply
Fires when A new Google review is posted about your business.

Every new Google review gets a reply, quickly and in your voice, whether it is five stars or one. Reviews sitting there unanswered read as inattentive to the next person deciding whether to call you.

Included by decision. This one is a choice rather than a default — it comes out when this is true: Owner writes his own replies. That is not you, so it is in.

What you will see. A reply under each new review, usually the same day — warm and specific on a good one, calm and straight on a complaint — posted in your business name, with anything awkward run past you first.
